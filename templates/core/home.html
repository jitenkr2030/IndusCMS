IndusCMS

Build industry-specific business applications on one reusable platform.
IndusCMS combines CMS capabilities, business infrastructure, dynamic entities, relationships and workflow automation into a reusable foundation for industry-specific applications.

Platform Engines

Business Engine

Businesses, memberships, roles, permissions, settings and audit logging.

Dynamic Entity Engine

Create configurable entities, fields and records without a new Django model for every business object.

Workflow Engine

Define steps, transitions, instances and a persistent workflow history timeline.

Industry Templates

Future templates will combine entities, workflows, dashboards, reports and permissions into complete applications.
