Industry-first Business Platform

IndusCMS

Build industry-specific business applications on one reusable platform.
IndusCMS combines CMS capabilities, business engines and a dynamic entity system to create configurable applications for different industries.

Platform Engines

CMS Engine

Pages, menus, media and themes for managing business content.

Business Engine

Businesses, roles, permissions, memberships, settings and audit logging.

Dynamic Entity Engine

Define custom business entities and fields without creating a new Django model for every use case.

Industry Templates

Future templates will combine entities, workflows, dashboards and permissions into complete applications.

Dynamic Business Data

Entities

Customer, Product, Vehicle, Student, Booking, Property and other configurable business objects.

Fields

Text, number, decimal, boolean, date, datetime, email, URL, choice and JSON field types.

Records

Dynamic records with validation, pagination, update and soft-delete support.

Relationships

Connect dynamic entities using relationship definitions, validated record links and relationship option APIs.

Security

Business membership, role-based permissions and audit logging are built into the platform foundation.
